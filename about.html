ABOUT US

Who We Are

MPOROKOSO MUSIC AWARDS is an annual Zambian music awards platform dedicated to recognising, celebrating and promoting musical talent across the country.

Our Vision

To become a respected annual platform that celebrates excellence, creativity and achievement in Zambian music.

Our Mission

To recognise talented artists, encourage musical development, promote unity and give deserving musicians an opportunity to shine.

Our Founder

BOSS ONE MUMBA

Founder of MPOROKOSO MUSIC AWARDS.

Annual Celebration

The awards will take place every year, beginning with the 2027 edition. Each edition will have its own nominations, voting and winners while maintaining the same founder and awards identity.

Our Coverage

All ten provinces of Zambia 🇿🇲.
